\textbf{\large Abstract}
\end{center}
In these notes, we present an exposition of the framework of SEVANI
(System for the Estimation of Variance due to Nonresponse and Imputation) \cite{Beaumont2011},
developed by Statistics Canada.
In Section \ref{DomainTotalSEVANI},
we explain the SEVANI methodology for the Horvitz-Thompson estimator
(with composite-linear imputation) for domain totals,
as described in \cite{Beaumont2011}, and present its underlying theory in a series of lemmas. 
In Section \ref{DomainRatioSEVANI} and Section \ref{CalibrationDomainTotalSEVANI},
we present in the same manner extensions of SEVANI to domain ratio estimators and
calibration estimators for domain totals, respectively.
