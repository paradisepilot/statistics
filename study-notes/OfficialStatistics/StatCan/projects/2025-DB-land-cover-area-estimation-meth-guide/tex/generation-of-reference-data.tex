\section{Generation of reference data}

          %%%%% ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ %%%%%

The term reference data refers to the delineation of each selected sampling unit (which 
represents a 100-metre $\times$ 100-metre plot of land) into polygons each having a fixed 
reference land cover type. The delineations were manually performed by land cover and 
satellite Earth observation (SEO) image interpretation experts (hereinafter, delineators) 
based on high-resolution SEO images covering the sampling units acquired around the 
target period of calendar year 2020. A thorough delineation protocol was developed to help 
ensure the delineations would be conducted accurately and uniformly across different 
delineators. A team of 11 delineators was assembled for reference data generation. 
Delineators were also instructed to identify challenging sampling units, for which secondary 
review was conducted.
