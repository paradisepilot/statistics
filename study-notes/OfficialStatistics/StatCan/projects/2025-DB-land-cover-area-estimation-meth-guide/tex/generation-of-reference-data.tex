\section{Generation of reference data}

          %%%%% ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ %%%%%

The term reference data refers to the delineation of each selected sampling unit
(which represents a 100-metre $\times$ 100-metre plot of land)
into polygons each having a reference land cover type.
The delineations were manually performed by trained interpreters (hereinafter, delineators) 
using high-resolution Satellite Earth Observation (SEO) images
covering the sampling units acquired around the target period of calendar year 2020.
A delineation protocol was developed to ensure the delineations
would be conducted accurately and consistently across different delineators.
A team of 8 delineators participated in the reference data generation.
Delineators also identified the subset of selected sampling units
for which more scrutiny was deemed appropriate and
for which a secondary review was conducted.
