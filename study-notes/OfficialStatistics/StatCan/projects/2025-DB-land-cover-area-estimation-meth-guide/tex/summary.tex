\textbf{\large Summary}
\end{center}
This document outlines the methodology used by Statistics Canada’s Census of Environment 
(CoE) program to generate the area estimates by target geography and land cover type 
released on March 27, 2025 \cite{StatCanCoELandCoverArea2025}.
As had been advocated by a number of recent research articles
\cite{Gallego2004,Olofsson201442,GreenCongalton2020,Stehman2023ErrorMatrix},
a design-based approach was adopted for the advantage that it yields
almost unbiased point estimators as well as accompanying variance estimators.
The sampled population is the collection of grid cells
(100 metre $\times$ 100 metre tiles) obtained by partitioning
inland Canada\footnote{the entire terrestrial and freshwater extent of Canada}
according to one of the CoE reference geospatial grids.
The sampling design was stratified simple random sampling,
stratifying by the cross-tabulation of ecoprovinces and land cover types.
The CoE Land Cover Register (LCR) \cite{StatCanCoELCR2025}
was used for stratification.
Sample allocation decisions were guided by the objective of 
controlling the sampling uncertainty of estimators for high-priority target variables,
informed by the results of a series of proof-of-concept studies.
Reference data were generated by expert interpretation of
high-resolution satellite images of the selected grid cells/tiles
acquired around the target period (calendar year 2020).
Horvitz-Thompson point estimators and variance estimators were used 
respectively to produce the land cover area and associated variance estimates.
Calibration and raking were applied to enforce coherence
between the two data tables in the release \cite{StatCanCoELandCoverArea2025},
as well as with the known areas of the target geographies.
\end{minipage}
\end{center}

\vskip 1.5cm
Land cover is a component of the Asset accounts described
in the System of Environmental-Economic Accounting -- Central Framework (SEEA-CF).
Data tables on land cover have been produced on an occasional basis
as statistical tables and in analytical products
since the inception of Statistics Canada’s environmental statistics program.
Land cover is also a key variable in the delineation of ecosystem types
in the context of the System of Environmental-Economic Accounting – Ecosystem Accounting.
New land cover estimates released on March 27, 2025 \cite{StatCanCoELandCoverArea2025}
follow a new design-based methodological approach.
